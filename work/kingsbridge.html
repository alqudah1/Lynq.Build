The brief

Make each service easy to find.

Kingsbridge offers custom homes and commercial property management across the Greater Toronto Area. The website needed to explain both services and give each audience a relevant way to get in touch.

What LYNQ built

From first impression
to the right inquiry.

01

A clear opening

A homepage that names both services, identifies the region and presents two distinct actions.

02

Room for the details

Dedicated service pages explain the work. The custom-home page introduces home types and the design-and-build process.

03

A relevant next step

Separate inquiry paths let visitors start with a custom-home project or a commercial-management request.

Built for the mobile journey

Keep the next step within reach.

The mobile homepage brings the two service actions into view. The home inquiry then asks for project location, project type and timeline.

Screenshots show the completed website. No increase in inquiries or revenue has been measured for this case study.

An honest presentation

Concepts stay labelled as concepts.

The site includes architectural direction imagery. It explicitly identifies these images as concepts, rather than presenting them as completed building projects.
