The brief

A brand that only existed on Instagram.

Finding Amy rents out a custom-built photo booth — nicknamed "Amy" — for weddings and events across Amman. Before LYNQ, the entire business lived on a single Instagram page. No website, no booking system, no brand system beyond the booth itself.

The ask was simple to say and harder to do: build a site that felt like the actual product, not a generic rental-business template.

The approach

Build the site around the booth, not around a template.

  • A full brand system extracted from the client's Instagram and direct conversation — a burgundy-and-cream palette, paired with the client's own commercial typefaces once licensed.
  • A 3D, interactive intro built in Three.js: visitors see and tap Amy's actual booth to enter the site, rather than scrolling past a hero banner.
  • An "Amy OS" interface for browsing packages, booking, and past events — designed to feel like using a real product, not browsing a brochure.
  • Live booking synced to the client's real Google Calendar — aware that the business runs two physical booth units, so a date only shows as fully booked once both are reserved.
  • Full-resolution, mobile-first rendering, since almost every booking happens from a phone at the event itself.
The result

Live, booking real events.

Finding Amy is live at finding-amy.com on its own domain, with a working calendar that reflects real availability across both booth units — no more manually checking bookings before confirming a date.
