The 4-fix checklist

Most local shops don't lose customers to a competitor. They lose them online, before anyone walks in: wrong hours, a slow site, a bad review nobody answered, a phone that keeps ringing. Four fixes, about 10 minutes each. Tick them off as you go.

4 fixesAbout 40 minutesFree tools only
FIX 01

The real hours, everywhere

If Google says you're closed, people believe Google. Your hours have to match on every app a customer might check.

FIX 02

A site that opens in a second

People look you up on their phone, often standing outside. If the page is slow or the menu is hard to find, they leave before it loads.

FIX 03

Answer every review

New customers read the bad reviews first, and they read your reply. One calm answer can do more than ten good reviews.

A reply you can adapt: "Thanks for telling us, Dana. Waiting that long isn't okay, and that's on us. We've changed how we handle the morning rush. Come back and ask for me, your coffee is on us."

Never buy reviews or only ask people you know will be happy. It's against Google's rules and it can get reviews removed.

FIX 04

Let them order or book without calling

At your busiest hour, every call you can't pick up is an order that goes somewhere else.
