# Kingsbridge Group — Brand Direction Summary

Prepared for client presentation. This is the direction the website is built
around — nothing here is final until you and the client sign off.

## Positioning

**Built for generations.** Kingsbridge Group brings custom home building, full
renovations, architecture, interior design, and residential/commercial/mixed-use
property management under one accountable team — backed by more than 20 years
of the family's own real estate experience.

We are careful throughout the site to say Kingsbridge Group is **backed by**
20+ years of family experience — not that the company itself has operated that
long. That distinction is intentional and consistent site-wide.

## Visual direction — "Modern Estate Luxury" (finalized palette)

- **Palette** — deep charcoal (`#161616`), warm ivory (`#F5F1E8`), stone beige
  (`#C8BBA6`), and brushed bronze (`#A67C52`) used sparingly for labels,
  hovers, and small marks. Deliberately not the black-and-gold "luxury real
  estate" cliché. Contrast-verified against WCAG AA throughout.
- **Type** — Libre Caslon Display (editorial serif) for headlines, Inter
  (clean grotesque) for navigation, body copy, and UI. Reads closer to an
  architecture studio or hospitality brand than a listings site.
- **Layout** — large photography, generous whitespace, hairline rules instead
  of card shadows, asymmetric two-column moments (the homepage's Build/Manage
  split, alternating image position on the Services page).
- **Motion** — restrained: fade-up on scroll, 1px underline hovers, a subtle
  image crossfade in the hero. No parallax, no bounce, nothing gimmicky.
  Fully respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Site structure

Home → Services (3 disciplines: Custom Homes & Development, Renovations &
Design, Property Management) → Projects/Areas of Expertise (4 categories,
framed explicitly as design inspiration, not completed projects) → About
(Our Story / Our Philosophy / Our Approach / Why Kingsbridge) → Contact
(7 inquiry paths — Build My Dream Home, Renovate My Home, Residential
Property Management, Commercial Property Management, Mixed-Use Properties,
Development Inquiry, General Inquiry — each revealing its own relevant
question set).

## What's placeholder vs. real

- **Real:** all copy, positioning, structure, and the 20+ years family
  experience claim.
- **Placeholder, clearly marked:** photography (styled gradient blocks
  labelled with what real photo belongs there, framed as "Areas of
  Expertise / Design Inspiration" — explicitly not completed Kingsbridge
  projects), project profiles (4 category cards explicitly say "to be
  confirmed" / "will appear here once confirmed"), the logo (3 directions
  presented, none chosen — KB Monogram is the client's stated priority).

Nothing on the public pages claims a fact, project, number, or piece of
history that hasn't been confirmed.
