Kingsbridge Group — Internal Review

Three logo directions, for discussion before anything is finalized.

Each direction is shown on dark and light backgrounds, plus a small favicon/avatar treatment to confirm it holds up at signage and social-profile sizes. None of the three is final — this exists to align on a direction before commissioning refined artwork.

Direction A

KB Monogram

A tight, architectural monogram — letterforms only, no symbol. The rule beneath the letters is a quiet nod to an architectural drawing’s title block: precise, structural, unfussy. Reads clearly at signage scale and holds up shrunk to a favicon.

Direction C

Legacy / Connection Mark

An abstract glyph paired with the wordmark — two uneven vertical strokes joined by a single horizontal line, suggesting connection between generations without drawing a literal bridge. The asymmetry is intentional: one generation building on the last, not a mirror image.
