Property Management

Our services

An integrated approach to property management gives owners greater consistency, clearer accountability and closer oversight. Kingsbridge brings the essential services behind a property together under one experienced team, reducing the complexity and administrative burden of dealing with several separate providers.

01

Administration

The day-to-day responsibilities that keep a property organised and professionally run.

We take on the day-to-day administration of the property. Correspondence with tenants and owners runs through one office, maintenance and service requests are recorded and coordinated with the appropriate service providers, and ownership is kept informed as matters progress. Leases, records and property documentation stay organised and easy to retrieve.

  • Tenant and owner communication
  • Maintenance coordination
  • Lease administration and documentation
  • Property records and correspondence
  • Coordination with relevant agencies and service providers
  • Day-to-day administrative oversight
  • Access to external legal counsel when required

Kingsbridge does not provide legal services.

02

Accounting

Clear financial records and reporting, so owners always know where the property stands.

We keep the financial side of the property documented and in view. Invoices and property costs are reviewed and tracked against the property, supporting records are kept in order, and owners receive reporting that brings the property’s financial information together in one place.

  • Financial documentation and reporting
  • Expense and property-cost tracking
  • Organised, accessible records
  • Regular reporting to ownership
  • Coordination of property-related financial information

Kingsbridge provides property-management record-keeping and reporting, not accounting, audit or tax advice.

03

Leasing

The leasing administration behind every new tenancy, from qualifying applicants to the lease in place.

We handle the administrative side of leasing. Applications from prospective tenants are reviewed to determine whether each applicant qualifies, the paperwork and process behind a new lease are managed through to completion, and first and last month’s rent requirements are handled before the tenancy begins. Once a lease is in place, we administer it as part of the property’s day-to-day management.

  • Leasing administration
  • Tenant qualification
  • Application and supporting-document review
  • Lease paperwork and processing
  • First and last month’s rent requirements
  • Ongoing lease administration

Leasing decisions and lease terms remain with ownership.

04

Consulting

Property-management guidance shaped around the needs of each property.

Owners come to us when a property needs a clearer plan. We look at how it is managed today, identify where time, cost or effort is being lost, and set out practical options for improvement, so ownership can decide on the next step with a full picture in front of them.

  • Property-management strategy
  • Operational recommendations
  • Identification of management inefficiencies
  • Planning and prioritising improvements
  • Support for informed property decisions
