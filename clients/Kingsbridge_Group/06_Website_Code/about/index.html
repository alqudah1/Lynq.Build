About

The foundation of Kingsbridge is built on trust.

Our Story

Two decades before the name was on the door.

Kingsbridge Group is a new name, but not a new pursuit. For more than 20 years, our family has been building, renovating, and managing real estate — first for ourselves, then for the people who trusted us with their own.

Every property taught us something the last one didn’t. Every renovation, every tenant relationship, every long winter spent on a maintenance call added up to a way of working we now bring to every Kingsbridge project — whether it’s a family’s first custom home or a portfolio built over years.

Our Philosophy

A house is not the same thing as a home.

Anyone can build a structure to code and call it finished. We think about what happens after handoff — the birthdays, the renovations down the road, the years a family will spend inside the walls we built. That’s the standard we design and build to.

It shapes property management too. A managed property isn’t a line item on a spreadsheet — it’s someone’s investment, and often someone else’s home. We manage it the way we’d want ours managed.

Our Approach

One team, accountable end to end.

Development, design, and property management are usually handled by separate companies that hand a project off between them — and accountability gets lost in the handoff. Kingsbridge keeps all three under one roof, so the same team that designs a space is accountable for how it’s built, and the same standard that shapes a new build shapes how we manage a property for years afterward.
