about nour
"Syrian by roots. Canadian by home. In the kitchen, there's no difference — it all goes into the pot."

Nour grew up watching her mother cook for everyone who came through the door — relatives, neighbours, strangers who left as friends. The table was never small enough to say no.

Now based in Toronto, she brings those same recipes forward: kibbeh the way her grandmother made it, fattoush with whatever's freshest that week, musakhan with enough sumac to mean it. All made to order, all from scratch.
