About Nour

Made with love,
from scratch.

I'm Nour. I came to Toronto from Damascus 8 years ago, and the first thing I missed was my mother's cooking. The smell of cumin and bay leaf, the sound of onions hitting a hot pan, the way rice turns golden in ghee.

So I started cooking. First for friends. Then for their families. Now for yours. Everything I make is from scratch — no shortcuts, no frozen shortcuts, no flavour packets. Just real Syrian home cooking, the way it's supposed to be.

"Food is the one thing that makes you feel at home, even when you're far from it. That's all I'm trying to do — bring a little bit of home to your table." — Nour, North York · 2016 to now
