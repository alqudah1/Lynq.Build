Our Story

Three years.
Still excited.

Drift started because we were tired of mediocre coffee. Not bad coffee — mediocre coffee. The kind that's technically fine but doesn't make you feel anything.

We wanted something that made you slow down. So we found a narrow space on Dundas West, put in a La Marzocco, and started sourcing coffees that genuinely surprised us.

Three years later, we still get excited about every bag that comes through the door. That's the whole point.

"We buy coffee from farmers we've met and roasters we trust. Nothing else."
