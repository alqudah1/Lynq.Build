The game

Built around atmosphere.

01 — Narrative
A story that shifts under you
Every decision rewrites what came before. Vault's branching narrative has no fixed truth — only your truth, built from what you chose to remember.
02 — Puzzles
Logic and memory entwined
Over 60 hand-crafted puzzles that respond to your emotional state. The same door opens differently depending on what you've seen.
03 — Atmosphere
Sound-first design
Original score by Mercury Prize-nominated composer Faye Alvers. Adaptive audio that responds in real time — silence is a mechanic, not a gap.
04 — Replayability
Three endings. One truth.
No two playthroughs surface the same details. New players consistently discover things on a third run that veterans missed entirely.
