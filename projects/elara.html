About Elara

Twelve years.
One client at a time.

I'm Elara — a licensed aesthetician and hair artist with twelve years in private studios across Toronto and London. In 2021, I opened my own space in Rosedale.

I work with one client at a time. No receptionists, no overlapping appointments. You get the full space, the full focus, and the full session — never a rushed finish.

I specialize in colour work and skin-focused makeup. Most of my clients have been coming for three or more years. That relationship is everything to me.

— Elara Nasser
