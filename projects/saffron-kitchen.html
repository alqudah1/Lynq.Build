Our Story

A Family Recipe, Brought to Brampton

Saffron Kitchen was founded in 2012 by Chef Arjun Sharma, who brought his family's treasured recipes from Amritsar to share with the GTA. Every dish on our menu is a tribute to that tradition.

We source our spices fresh from trusted suppliers and prepare everything from scratch daily. No shortcuts, no compromises — just honest, flavourful food made with love.
