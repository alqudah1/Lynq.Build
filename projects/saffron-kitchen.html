Our Story

A Family Recipe,
Brought to Toronto

Saffron Kitchen was born from Chef Arjun Sharma's dream — to bring his family's treasured recipes from Amritsar to the people of Toronto. Every dish on our menu is a tribute to that tradition, unchanged for generations.

We source our spices fresh from trusted suppliers and prepare everything from scratch each morning. No shortcuts, no compromises — just honest, deeply flavourful food made with love.

Whether it's a quiet dinner, a birthday celebration, or catering for 200 guests, we bring the same dedication to every plate we serve.

Arjun Sharma
