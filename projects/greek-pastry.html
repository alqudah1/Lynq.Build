OUR STORY

A taste of Greece,
on a Barceloneta corner.

We come from a family of bakers in Athens. Our grandmother taught our mother the way of phyllo — thirty-six layers, paper-thin, folded by hand — and our mother taught us. Then we came to Barcelona, fell in love with the city, and decided to bring what we know.

Every morning before dawn we make the dough from scratch. We press the phyllo, fill it with feta and spinach or apricot and walnut, and bake it the way it's been done in our family kitchen for generations.

The baklava is soaked in Greek honey — real honey, from home. The bougatsa is still warm when you walk in. And the frappé? Shaken hard, poured cold, and served in a tall glass with a long straw. Just like in Athens.
