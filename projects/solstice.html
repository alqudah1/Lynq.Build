The studio

A place to arrive,
not perform.

"Yoga isn't about touching your toes. It's about what you learn on the way down."

Solstice opened in 2018 in the Annex with twelve mats, a heated room, and a simple principle: practice is for you, not an audience. No mirrors, no leaderboards, no livestreams.

We have four resident teachers with a combined 60 years of practice. Small class sizes (max 14) mean you get real guidance, real adjustment, and real silence when you need it.
