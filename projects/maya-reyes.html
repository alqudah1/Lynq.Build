About

I photograph
people as they are.

I'm Maya — a portrait and editorial photographer based in Toronto, working wherever the light is right. My work is about stillness. Finding the second before the mask goes back on.

I've shot for Lux, The Sunday Times, and independent labels across North America and Europe. I'm drawn to natural light, quiet rooms, and faces that have a story.

Available for portrait commissions, editorial, events, and campaign work. I travel for the right project.
