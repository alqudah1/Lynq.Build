OUR FAMILY

Two coasts,
one kitchen.

Cem grew up in Bandırma, a port town on the Sea of Marmara, watching his father cook for everyone who walked through the door. The mezze, the koftes, the slow-grilled fish — it was the language of the house.

When they opened SMYRNA in Barceloneta, they kept that language and added another. Spanish paella, fresh-caught seafood from the Boqueria, Padrón peppers from Galicia. Two coastlines, one menu.

The kitchen is run by father and son. The terrace looks out over the water. And every dish — from the first piece of bread to the last shot of raki — comes out of the same idea: feed people the way you'd feed your own family.

THE TERRACE

Sunset over the marina.

Our outdoor terrace faces the Port Vell marina. Heated in winter, shaded in summer, open year-round. From late afternoon, it's the best spot in Barceloneta to watch the boats come in with a cold drink in hand.
