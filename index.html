What it is

Your company's operating workspace

Projects, CRM, tasks, approvals, marketing, communications, reporting, and team access are organized around how your company actually works.

What we automate

Repeatable work across the business

Examples include intake, lead qualification, routing, follow-up drafts, reminders, status updates, document preparation, recurring reports, and on-brand posts, carousels and reels drafted in Content Studio.

Where savings come from

Less admin and fewer disconnected tools

The Office can consolidate workflows, reduce copying between systems, shorten handoffs, and prevent opportunities or approvals from being forgotten.
