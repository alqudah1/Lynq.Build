Core capabilities

The operating layers growing companies usually piece together.

CRM and pipeline

Companies, contacts, opportunities, activity, source attribution, ownership, and next actions.

DELIVERY

Projects and work

Briefs, phases, tasks, dependencies, files, status, team handoffs, and founder or client approvals.

GROWTH

Marketing and reporting

Campaigns, content calendars, links, channel outcomes, leads, conversion context, and performance reviews.

COMMUNICATION

Email and follow-up

Tracked conversations, approved drafts, reminders, customer history, and clear human ownership.

AUTOMATION

Repeatable workflows

Intake, qualification, routing, document preparation, alerts, summaries, and recurring operational updates.

CONTROL

Roles and approvals

Company access, department permissions, audit history, approval gates, and limits on what automation can do.
